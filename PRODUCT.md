# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Spine surgeons, OR staff, and hospital procurement in Austria and the DACH region. They visit on desktop at work to research implants and contact the distributor; on mobile mostly to quickly check contacts, look up a product, or share a page with a colleague.

## Product Purpose

B2B marketing and product catalog website for SpineLine – Andreas Kendlbacher GmbH, an Austrian distributor of European spinal surgical implants (cervical, thoracolumbar, lumbar cages, PediGuard, universal). The site must communicate expertise, build trust with surgeons, and drive direct contact (phone, email, form).

## Brand Personality

Professionell, vertrauenswürdig, präzise. Premium but approachable. Austrian directness without corporate coldness. Gold accent (#d3aa04) against dark warm brown signals quality without shouting.

## Anti-references

- Generic medical product catalogs with no personality
- SaaS-cream layouts with hero metrics and identical card grids
- Busy clinical sites that look like a hospital intranet
- Bottom-nav app shells (this is a website, not an app)

## Design Principles

1. **Precision over decoration** – every element earns its place; remove what doesn't communicate.
2. **Trust through detail** – real photos, real names, real phone numbers; no stock-photo anonymity.
3. **European quality signals** – materials, spacing, and typography should feel premium, not clinical.
4. **Mobile as a lookup tool** – on small screens, prioritize contact info, product names, and brochure downloads above everything else.
5. **Consistent hierarchy** – same visual language across index and all five product subpages.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Body text ≥16px on mobile. Touch targets ≥44×44px. Hamburger menu already implemented; verify it is keyboard-accessible.
